# SLICE-01 Data Model

This document lists the exact fields and constraints of the target PostgreSQL schema. It is the
source for `prisma/schema.prisma` and migration `0001_init` (task S1-P02-T002/T003). Relationships
and diagrams are in `erd.md`.

Status of every entity: **[proposed]** until migration `0001_init` exists. They are derived from the brief, CLAUDE.md, and ADR-006…011
(approved 2026-09-15, gate S1-P01-T010). Entities that exist in the baseline schema are marked *(baseline)*. Anything that
depends on an unresolved open question says so.

---

## 1. Conventions

### 1.1 Naming — no contradictions between layers

| Layer | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptual entity (docs) | UPPER_SNAKE singular | `ORDER_ITEM` |
| PostgreSQL table | snake_case plural (`@@map`) | `order_items` |
| PostgreSQL column | snake_case (`@map`) | `tenant_id` |
| Prisma model | PascalCase singular | `OrderItem` |
| Prisma / TypeScript field | camelCase | `tenantId` |

`tenant_id` (column), `tenantId` (TypeScript) and "tenant" (concept) are the same attribute.
This convention is already used in the baseline (`prisma/schema.prisma` uses `@map("tenant_id")`) [fact].

### 1.2 Types

| Kind | PostgreSQL type | Prisma | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identifier | `UUID` | `String @db.Uuid @default(dbgenerated("gen_random_uuid()"))` | Generated by PostgreSQL; never sequential |
| Money | `NUMERIC(12,2)` | `Decimal @db.Decimal(12,2)` | CLAUDE.md rule 4; `Prisma.Decimal` arithmetic only (ADR-010) |
| Rate (percent) | `NUMERIC(5,2)` | `Decimal @db.Decimal(5,2)` | 0.00–100.00 |
| Timestamp | `TIMESTAMPTZ(6)` | `DateTime @db.Timestamptz(6)` | Stored UTC; displayed in `RESTAURANT.timezone` |
| Business date | `DATE` | `DateTime @db.Date` | Calendar date in restaurant timezone (ADR-010 §5) |
| Short text | `VARCHAR(n)` | `String @db.VarChar(n)` | Length enforced by DB and Zod |
| Local wall-clock time | `TIME(0)` | `DateTime @db.Time(0)` | Opening hours only; minute precision, no time zone (interpreted in `RESTAURANT.timezone`) |
| Structured document | `JSONB` | `Json` | Only for non-monetary, non-relational payloads (print render doc, redacted audit state) |

### 1.3 Standard columns

| Column | Type | Req | Default | Meaning | Validation | Security | Index | Unique | FK | Tenant scoped | Audit impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | UUID | Y | `gen_random_uuid()` | Primary key | UUID v4 format on input | IDs are not secrets; access is by scope, not obscurity | PK | PK | — | — | Recorded as `resource_id` |
| `tenant_id` | UUID | Y (tenant-owned) | — | Owning tenant | Never read from client input | Set only from `TenantContext`/`AgentContext` (ADR-006/008) | Leading column of every tenant index | Part of `(tenant_id, id)` | `tenants.id` `ON DELETE RESTRICT` | Yes | Recorded as `audit_logs.tenant_id` |
| `created_at` | TIMESTAMPTZ | Y | `now()` | Creation instant (UTC) | Server-set | — | Where listed | — | — | — | — |
| `updated_at` | TIMESTAMPTZ | Y | `now()`, `@updatedAt` | Last change instant; polling cursor (ADR-009) | Server-set | — | `(tenant_id, updated_at)` where polled | — | — | — | — |
| `archived_at` | TIMESTAMPTZ | N | NULL | Soft-archive instant; archived rows are hidden from operational lists and public site but kept for history | Server-set | Archive, not delete, for anything an order may reference | Partial indexes `WHERE archived_at IS NULL` | — | — | — | `*.archived` event |

### 1.4 Deletion policy

- **Never hard-deleted:** tenants, users, memberships, menu categories/items/variants/add-ons, customers,
  orders and all order children, KOTs, transactions, print jobs, audit logs. They use status or `archived_at`.
- **Superseded in part by RASOIOS-ADR-021 (2026-10-06):** finished orders and their children, KOTs, transactions,
  day closes, print jobs, customers without orders, staff attendance, social posts and audit entries *before a date* may
  be deleted by the owner/administrator, only after a matching backup (ADR-021 §3–4). `data.*` audit rows are never deleted.
- **Hard-deletable:** `DAILY_MENU` in `DRAFT` (cascades to its items), `RESTAURANT_HOURS` rows (replaced as a set),
  `RATE_LIMIT_BUCKET` rows (expiry), `TENANT_COUNTER` never.
- All tenant FKs are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

### 1.5 Tenant integrity (ADR-008)

Every table marked *Tenant scoped = Yes* declares `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)` (exempt: `audit_logs`, whose `tenant_id` is nullable and which nothing references; `tenant_counters`, keyed by `(tenant_id, counter_type, business_date)`). Every child references its parent with a
composite FK `(tenant_id, parent_id) → parent(tenant_id, id)`, so a row cannot reference another tenant's parent.

## 1. Conventions

### 1.1 Naming — no contradictions between layers

| Layer | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptual entity (docs) | UPPER_SNAKE singular | `ORDER_ITEM` |
| PostgreSQL table | snake_case plural (`@@map`) | `order_items` |

### 1.4 Deletion policy

- **Never hard-deleted:** tenants, users, memberships, menu categories/items/variants/add-ons, customers,
  orders and all order children, KOTs, transactions, print jobs, audit logs. They use status or `archived_at`.
- **Hard-deletable:** `DAILY_MENU` in `DRAFT` (cascades to its items), `RESTAURANT_HOURS` rows (replaced as a set),
  `RATE_LIMIT_BUCKET` rows (expiry), `TENANT_COUNTER` never.
- All tenant FKs are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

### 1.5 Tenant integrity (ADR-008)

Every table marked *Tenant scoped = Yes* declares `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)` (exempt: `audit_logs`, whose `tenant_id` is nullable and which nothing references; `tenant_counters`, keyed by `(tenant_id, counter_type, business_date)`). Every child references its parent with a
composite FK `(tenant_id, parent_id) → parent(tenant_id, id)`, so a row cannot reference another tenant's parent.

### 1.4 Deletion policy

- **Never hard-deleted:** tenants, users, memberships, menu categories/items/variants/add-ons, customers,
  orders and all order children, KOTs, transactions, print jobs, audit logs. They use status or `archived_at`.
- **Hard-deletable:** `DAILY_MENU` in `DRAFT` (cascades to its items), `RESTAURANT_HOURS` rows (replaced as a set),
  `RATE_LIMIT_BUCKET` rows (expiry), `TENANT_COUNTER` never.
- All tenant FKs are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

### 1.5 Tenant integrity (ADR-008)

composite FK `(tenant_id, parent_id) → parent(tenant_id, id)`, so a row cannot reference another tenant's parent.

## 1. Conventions

### 1.1 Naming — no contradictions between layers

| Layer | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptual entity (docs) | UPPER_SNAKE singular | `ORDER_ITEM` |
| PostgreSQL table | snake_case plural (`@@map`) | `order_items` |

| Prisma model | PascalCase singular | `OrderItem` |
| Prisma / TypeScript field | camelCase | `tenantId` |

`tenant_id` (column), `tenantId` (TypeScript) and "tenant" (concept) are the same attribute.

### 1.2 Types

| Kind | PostgreSQL type | Prisma | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|

| Money | `NUMERIC(12,2)` | `Decimal @db.Decimal(12,2)` | CLAUDE.md rule 4; `Prisma.Decimal` arithmetic only (ADR-010) |

### 1.4 Deletion policy

- **Never hard-deleted:** tenants, users, memberships, menu categories/items/variants/add-ons, customers,
  orders and all order children, KOTs, transactions, print jobs, audit logs. They use status or `archived_at`.
- **Hard-deletable:** `DAILY_MENU` in `DRAFT` (cascades to its items), `RESTAURANT_HOURS` rows (replaced as a set),
  `RATE_LIMIT_BUCKET` rows (expiry), `TENANT_COUNTER` never.
- All tenant FKs are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

### 1.5 Tenant integrity (ADR-008)

Every table marked *Tenant scoped = Yes* declares `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)` (exempt: `audit_logs`, whose `tenant_id` is nullable and which nothing references; `tenant_counters`, keyed by `(tenant_id, counter_type, business_date)`). Every child references its parent with a
composite FK `(tenant_id, parent_id) → parent(tenant_id, id)`, so a row cannot reference another tenant's parent.

# SLICE-01 Data Model

This document lists the exact fields and constraints of the target PostgreSQL schema. It is the
source for `prisma/schema.prisma` and migration `0001_init` (task S1-P02-T002/T003). Relationships
and diagrams are in `erd.md`.

Status of every entity: **[proposed]** until migration `0001_init` exists. They are derived from the brief, CLAUDE.md, and ADR-006…011
(approved 2026-09-15, gate S1-P01-T010). Entities that exist in the baseline schema are marked *(baseline)*. Anything that
depends on an unresolved open question says so.

## 1. Conventions

### 1.1 Naming — no contradictions between layers

| Layer | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptual entity (docs) | UPPER_SNAKE singular | `ORDER_ITEM` |
| PostgreSQL table | snake_case plural (`@@map`) | `order_items` |
| PostgreSQL column | snake_case (`@map`) | `tenant_id` |
| Prisma model | PascalCase singular | `OrderItem` |
| Prisma / TypeScript field | camelCase | `tenantId` |

`tenant_id` (column), `tenantId` (TypeScript) and "tenant" (concept) are the same attribute.
This convention is already used in the baseline (`prisma/schema.prisma` uses `@map("tenant_id")`) [fact].

### 1.2 Types

| Kind | PostgreSQL type | Prisma | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identifier | `UUID` | `String @db.Uuid @default(dbgenerated("gen_random_uuid()"))` | Generated by PostgreSQL; never sequential |
| Money | `NUMERIC(12,2)` | `Decimal @db.Decimal(12,2)` | CLAUDE.md rule 4; `Prisma.Decimal` arithmetic only (ADR-010) |
| Rate (percent) | `NUMERIC(5,2)` | `Decimal @db.Decimal(5,2)` | 0.00–100.00 |
| Timestamp | `TIMESTAMPTZ(6)` | `DateTime @db.Timestamptz(6)` | Stored UTC; displayed in `RESTAURANT.timezone` |
| Business date | `DATE` | `DateTime @db.Date` | Calendar date in restaurant timezone (ADR-010 §5) |
| Short text | `VARCHAR(n)` | `String @db.VarChar(n)` | Length enforced by DB and Zod |
| Local wall-clock time | `TIME(0)` | `DateTime @db.Time(0)` | Opening hours only; minute precision, no time zone (interpreted in `RESTAURANT.timezone`) |
| Structured document | `JSONB` | `Json` | Only for non-monetary, non-relational payloads (print render doc, redacted audit state) |

### 1.4 Deletion policy

- **Never hard-deleted:** tenants, users, memberships, menu categories/items/variants/add-ons, customers,
  orders and all order children, KOTs, transactions, print jobs, audit logs. They use status or `archived_at`.
- **Hard-deletable:** `DAILY_MENU` in `DRAFT` (cascades to its items), `RESTAURANT_HOURS` rows (replaced as a set),
  `RATE_LIMIT_BUCKET` rows (expiry), `TENANT_COUNTER` never.
- All tenant FKs are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

### 1.5 Tenant integrity (ADR-008)

Every table marked *Tenant scoped = Yes* declares `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)` (exempt: `audit_logs`, whose `tenant_id` is nullable and which nothing references; `tenant_counters`, keyed by `(tenant_id, counter_type, business_date)`). Every child references its parent with a
composite FK `(tenant_id, parent_id) → parent(tenant_id, id)`, so a row cannot reference another tenant's parent.

### 3.1 Additional database constraints

`0001_init` also enforces these defensive CHECKs, which are stricter than the dictionary rows above and never contradict them
(catalogue test TC-DB-005 has a negative case for every CHECK in the database): `orders` paid/refunded/version ≥ 0 and
`currency_code ~ '^[A-Z]{3}`; `order_items` add-ons total and line tax ≥ 0; `kot_items.quantity` 1–99; `transactions`
`change_due ≥ 0`, PAYMENT rows have no `refund_of_transaction_id`, SUCCESS rows have no `voided_at`; `business_day_closes`
`cash_variance = counted_cash − expected_cash`, refund total and counts ≥ 0.
